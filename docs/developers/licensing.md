Smart Parks publishes OpenCollar under open licences chosen per kind of work, as stated in the RangerEdge documentation repository:

| Kind of work | Licence |
|---|---|
| Hardware source: PCB design files, schematics, BOM, manufacturing files, mechanical and CAD designs | [CERN-OHL-W-2.0](https://ohwr.org/cern_ohl_w_v2.txt) |
| Firmware and software | [MPL-2.0](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/2.0/) (the public firmware repository); individual tools use MIT or GPL-3.0, see the [repository index](repositories.md) |
| Documentation, including this site | [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) |

Where a repository carries its own LICENSE file, that file prevails. Many repositories do not yet carry a licence file; adding one is part of the ongoing cleanup and until then the statement above reflects the intended licensing.

The scripts and workflows in the [opencollar repository](https://github.com/SmartParksOrg/opencollar) are MIT licensed.
