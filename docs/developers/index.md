# Developers

OpenCollar spans about forty repositories in the [SmartParksOrg](https://github.com/SmartParksOrg) organization: firmware, hardware, mechanics, per-device documentation, tools and decoders. This section is the map.

- [Repositories](repositories.md): every repository with its purpose, status, licence and latest release.
- [Contributing](contributing.md): where to report issues and how to propose changes.
- [Licensing and trademarks](licensing.md): which licence applies to hardware, firmware and documentation.

## Repository naming

Historic names follow the pattern `smartparks-<device>-<kind>` with `kind` one of `master` (documentation, BOM and assembly), `hardware` (electronics) and `mechanics`. Tool repositories have their own names. Some device names differ between repositories and products: `rhinopuck35` is sold as RhinoEdge Puck 34, `freeedge` is the board of the CollarEdge Free, and `fence_monitor` is the FenceEdge. This site uses the product names and keeps the repository names underneath.

## Development repositories that are private

The firmware development repository, the Android Connect App and the provisioning software are private. Their releases are public: firmware through the public mirror, the app through Google Play.
